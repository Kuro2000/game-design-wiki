# Choke Points

**Summary**: Bottleneck variables in a [[core-gameplay-loop]] that cap how fast a player can progress. They are simultaneously the design's pacing tool and its monetization surface.

**Sources**: `Making a Hit Idle Game – Eight Lessons from Kolibri Games.md`.

**Last updated**: 2026-04-25.

---

## Definition

Between each stage of [[idle-miner-tycoon]]'s loop sits a "choke point — a variable that determines how quickly you can progress" (source: Making a Hit Idle Game – Eight Lessons from Kolibri Games.md). Upgrading a choke point widens the bottleneck (mine faster, transport faster, sell for more).

## Why they matter

- **Diagnostic.** Players who can see the choke point know what to upgrade next, which feeds the "just one more upgrade" pull.
- **Monetizable.** Each choke point is a place [[pay-or-wait-monetization]] can attach: spend to widen it now, or wait.
- **Tunable.** Choke points are knobs designers can turn to adjust pacing without changing the loop itself.
