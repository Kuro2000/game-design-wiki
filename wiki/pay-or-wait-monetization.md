# Pay-or-Wait Monetization

**Summary**: The free-to-play monetization model native to [[idle-games]]: players can't lose, only progress slowly, so the spend prompt is "skip the wait" rather than "avoid failure."

**Sources**: `Making a Hit Idle Game – Eight Lessons from Kolibri Games.md`.

**Last updated**: 2026-04-25.

---

## The model

> "You can't lose an idle game. It's all about how fast you want to progress. And that speed of progression is what players will pay to boost."
> — Nate Barker, [[kolibri-games]] (source: Making a Hit Idle Game – Eight Lessons from Kolibri Games.md)

At each [[choke-points|choke point]] in the [[core-gameplay-loop]], players are offered two options:

1. **Pay** — spend in-game currency, real money, or watch a [[rewarded-ads|rewarded ad]] to skip ahead.
2. **Wait** — keep playing the normal loop until the bottleneck clears.

## Why it works for idle games

- The genre is loss-free, so monetization can't lean on the "avoid death" pressure most F2P games use.
- The [[choke-points]] are visible and frequent, giving many natural prompts.
- Time-to-next-upgrade is the player's mental currency; price discrimination on time is intuitive.

## Revenue mix (Kolibri)

The source contains a contradictory revenue split (60% ads + 60% IAP = 120%). Both channels are clearly material, but the exact split needs verification — see note in [[making-a-hit-idle-game-kolibri]].

## Community-driven IAP

Notably, IAPs were not in [[idle-miner-tycoon]]'s MVP — they were added because players asked for a way to spend money to progress faster. A reminder that monetization channels can be missed if the team isn't in dialogue with its community.
