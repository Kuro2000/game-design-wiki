# Core Gameplay Loop

**Summary**: The short, repeatable cycle of actions that constitutes the moment-to-moment play of a game. In [[idle-games]], best practice is to make every step of the loop visible on a single screen.

**Sources**: `Making a Hit Idle Game – Eight Lessons from Kolibri Games.md`.

**Last updated**: 2026-04-25.

---

## Why one-screen visibility matters

[[idle-miner-tycoon]] shows all four loop stages — mine, transport, sell, upgrade — on one screen. Two consequences (source: Making a Hit Idle Game – Eight Lessons from Kolibri Games.md):

1. **Accessibility.** A player can pick the game up on a 10-minute commute and immediately understand what to do.
2. **Monetization legibility.** When the loop is visible, so are its [[choke-points]] — and choke points are where the [[pay-or-wait-monetization]] model attaches.

## General principle

The loop should be:

- **Short** enough to repeat many times per session.
- **Visible** so players self-teach the mechanics.
- **Gated** by clear bottlenecks the player can identify and target.
