# Kolibri Games

**Summary**: German mobile games studio (Frankfurt-area roots) that built the *Idle ___ Tycoon* franchise. Grew from 4 people in a student apartment to 100+ employees and 100M+ downloads.

**Sources**: `Making a Hit Idle Game – Eight Lessons from Kolibri Games.md`.

**Last updated**: 2026-04-25.

---

## Origin

Founded in 2016 as a four-person team near Frankfurt. Their first attempted game, **Front Yard Wars**, was scrapped at roughly 60% completion (source: Making a Hit Idle Game – Eight Lessons from Kolibri Games.md). The retrospective named three failure modes: waterfall development, an over-complex technical pipeline, and no playtesting for months. Willingness to kill that project became the template for [[lean-development]] inside the studio.

## Games covered in the wiki

- [[idle-miner-tycoon]] (2016) — debut hit.
- [[idle-factory-tycoon]] (2018) — second title using the refined process.

## What they're known for

- Tight [[mvp-prototyping]] discipline ("say no to everything you want to do").
- Weekly update cadence with [[staged-rollout]].
- Heavy use of [[rewarded-ads]] alongside IAPs in their [[pay-or-wait-monetization]] model.
- Listening loop with their player community as a primary product input.
