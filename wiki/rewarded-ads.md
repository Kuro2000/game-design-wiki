# Rewarded Ads

**Summary**: Opt-in video ads that players watch in exchange for an in-game benefit. The dominant ad format in mobile [[idle-games]] because it aligns with the [[pay-or-wait-monetization]] loop.

**Sources**: `Making a Hit Idle Game – Eight Lessons from Kolibri Games.md`.

**Last updated**: 2026-04-25.

---

## Behaviour at Kolibri

In [[idle-miner-tycoon]] (source: Making a Hit Idle Game – Eight Lessons from Kolibri Games.md):

- Average **8 ads watched per player per session per day**.
- Ad engagement rate **52%**.
- Largest single use case: watching an ad to boost a mine's revenue.

## Why it fits idle games

- Each ad watch is functionally identical to a small [[pay-or-wait-monetization|"pay"]] — it accelerates the next [[choke-points|choke point]] without spending money.
- Because the player chose to watch, the ad doesn't feel intrusive in the way an interstitial would.
- It captures revenue from the large segment of players who will never make an IAP.
