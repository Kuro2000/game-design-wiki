# MVP Prototyping

**Summary**: Build the smallest playable version that can validate (or invalidate) the core game-fit hypothesis. The discipline is in what you *don't* build.

**Sources**: `Making a Hit Idle Game – Eight Lessons from Kolibri Games.md`.

**Last updated**: 2026-04-25.

---

## The Kolibri MVP

The [[idle-miner-tycoon]] prototype shipped without (source: Making a Hit Idle Game – Eight Lessons from Kolibri Games.md):

- 3D art
- in-app purchases
- social features
- localization
- ad mediation
- long-term motivation
- cloudsave

It still hit 63–81% D1 retention — well above the ~40% baseline. See [[d1-retention]].

## The motto

> "Say no to everything you want to do."
> — Nate Barker, [[kolibri-games]]

The point isn't asceticism; it's protecting the signal. With fewer variables in play, a strong [[d1-retention]] number is unambiguously a sign that the core mechanic works. Polish, monetization, and meta systems can be layered on later without invalidating that signal.

## Why this works for idle games specifically

[[idle-games]] live or die on the feel of the [[core-gameplay-loop]]. That loop is cheap to build and the retention signal arrives fast — so MVP is a near-perfect fit for the genre.
