# Live Ops Features

**Summary**: Post-launch content and systems that keep an [[idle-games|idle game]] (or any live game) compelling for years. Drawn from [[idle-miner-tycoon]]'s 160+ updates.

**Sources**: `Making a Hit Idle Game – Eight Lessons from Kolibri Games.md`.

**Last updated**: 2026-04-25.

---

## Cadence

[[kolibri-games]] aim for one update per week, released via [[staged-rollout]]. Frequent updates train players to expect new content and keep the game in their rotation (source: Making a Hit Idle Game – Eight Lessons from Kolibri Games.md).

## Categories of features added post-launch

- **New resources to mine.** Started with realistic materials (coal, gold), expanded into playful ones like candy once the realistic set was exhausted.
- **New areas / world map.** Added a layer of geographic exploration to a single-screen game.
- **Expeditions.** First social feature — players cooperate to unlock rewards faster.
- **Expandable skill trees.** Where the metagame began; player-directed power-ups beyond the [[core-gameplay-loop]].
- **Event mines.** Limited-time mines with novelty resources (sushi, aliens, pearls). Often **doubled weekend revenue.**
- **Season pass.** Adopted after seeing it work in Clash of Clans and Fortnite. Boosts event-mine rewards for a fixed period. Described as "huge for our revenue."

## Pattern

Roughly: realistic-to-novel content expansion → spatial/exploration layer → first social hook → metagame depth → time-limited event content → recurring battle-pass-style monetization. A reasonable template for sequencing live-ops on an idle game.
