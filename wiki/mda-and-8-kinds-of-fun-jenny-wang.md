# MDA & 8 Kinds of Fun (Jenny Wang on Zelda BotW)

**Summary**: Jenny Wang's Medium article applies the [[eight-kinds-of-fun|8 aesthetics]] from the [[mda-framework]] to *The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild* — a useful worked example of how to do an aesthetic decomposition in practice.

**Sources**: `MDA & 8 Kinds of Fun.md` (Medium, 2020-09-24).

**Last updated**: 2026-04-25.

---

## What the article does

It takes a single game (BotW) and walks through how each of the [[eight-kinds-of-fun]] shows up in it. The article is informal but useful as a template for how to read games through MDA.

## The aesthetics Wang identifies in BotW

| Aesthetic | Where it appears |
|---|---|
| **Narrative** | Link's quest to defeat Ganon and rescue Zelda; the 100-years-ago backstory revealed through memories. |
| **Fantasy** | Magic, monsters, alternate world — the game is a fantasy by definition. |
| **Challenge** | Four Divine Beasts before the final Ganon fight; 120 shrines as smaller challenges. |
| **Discovery** | Hidden side villages, side quests, building a house, witnessing marriages — far more content than the main path requires. |
| **Expression** | No prescribed playstyle: players self-direct toward combat, shrines, cooking, costume collection, etc. |
| **Sensation** | The sheer beauty of the world — riding a horse across the Plateau as a "feels like actual wilderness" moment. |

(Source: MDA & 8 Kinds of Fun.md.)

## What's missing

Wang doesn't explicitly address [[eight-kinds-of-fun|Fellowship or Submission]] in BotW. Both are arguably weak in BotW (single-player; not particularly grindy/repetitive in the Submission sense), which itself is informative — a game's *missing* aesthetics characterise it as much as its present ones.

## Why this article is useful for the wiki

It's a concrete demonstration that the MDA aesthetics aren't just academic categories — they map cleanly onto how a player describes what makes a game enjoyable. The exercise of doing this decomposition is itself a design tool: you can plot your own work-in-progress against the same eight axes and identify weaknesses.
