# Idle Miner Tycoon

**Summary**: [[kolibri-games]]' debut hit, released July 2016. An idle business sim where players dig downward instead of building upward — a small twist on the standard tower-builder shape that was enough to differentiate in a crowded market.

**Sources**: `Making a Hit Idle Game – Eight Lessons from Kolibri Games.md`.

**Last updated**: 2026-04-25.

---

## Differentiation

The team deliberately picked the under-used "build downward" framing rather than another tower-builder or factory sim. A small differentiator was enough to break out of the saturated [[idle-games]] market (source: Making a Hit Idle Game – Eight Lessons from Kolibri Games.md).

## Core loop

Four stages, all visible on one screen — see [[core-gameplay-loop]]:

1. **Mine** resources.
2. **Transport** them upward.
3. **Sell** at the surface.
4. **Upgrade** any of the above.

Between each stage sits a [[choke-points|choke point]] that determines throughput.

## MVP

The launch prototype shipped without 3D art, IAP, social features, localization, ad mediation, long-term motivation, or cloudsave. It still hit D1 retention of 63–81% — see [[mvp-prototyping]] and [[d1-retention]].

## Monetization

[[pay-or-wait-monetization]] with both [[rewarded-ads]] and IAP. (See note in [[making-a-hit-idle-game-kolibri]] about the inconsistent revenue-split numbers in the source.)

## Live ops & longevity

160+ updates since launch, ~1 per week. Major additions: new resources (coal → candy), world map, expeditions (first social feature), skill trees, event mines, season pass — see [[live-ops-features]].
