# Idle Factory Tycoon

**Summary**: [[kolibri-games]]' second idle title, released 2018. Built using the refined five-step process the studio developed after [[idle-miner-tycoon]].

**Sources**: `Making a Hit Idle Game – Eight Lessons from Kolibri Games.md`.

**Last updated**: 2026-04-25.

---

## Why it matters in this wiki

The source documents the *process* used to build it more than the game itself. This is the title where Kolibri formalised their [[lean-development]] pipeline (source: Making a Hit Idle Game – Eight Lessons from Kolibri Games.md):

1. Small team finds a concept and core mechanic.
2. Build a simple prototype, test, refine.
3. Internal team-wide playtest (~8 weeks in).
4. External testing — early-day retention, organic downloads, ratings, feedback (~4 weeks).
5. Scale up team and features.
