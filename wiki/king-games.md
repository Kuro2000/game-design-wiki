# King

**Summary**: Stockholm-founded mobile games studio (now part of Activision Blizzard) best known for the [[candy-crush-franchise]]. Operates studios across Europe including Stockholm, Berlin, and London. Heavy focus on the [[saga-games|Saga]] format.

**Sources**: `Level Design Saga - Jeremy Kang.md`; `GDC2020 Blockers - Lucien Chen.md`.

**Last updated**: 2026-04-25.

---

## What they make

The [[candy-crush-franchise]] is the flagship — multiple Saga titles built on [[match-3-games|match-3]] mechanics. Other King titles in the Saga format that get passing mention: Bubble Witch Saga, Farm Heroes Saga, Pet Rescue Saga.

Jeremy Kang's 2016 talk also references **Lucky Lantern**, an in-development non-match-3 Saga (link 3+ lanterns to clear them; link 6+ to summon a dragon) used as the running example throughout [[level-design-saga-jeremy-kang]].

## What they're known for in this wiki

- Formalised [[level-design]] practice for [[saga-games]] — see [[level-design-saga-jeremy-kang]].
- The [[blocker-framework]] for analysing match-3 difficulty — see [[gdc2020-blockers-lucien-chen]].
- Heavy investment in [[level-testing]] and KPIs (difficulty, retention, progression, monetisation).
- Internal knowledge sharing — designers play each other's games; regular meetups across studios (source: Level Design Saga - Jeremy Kang.md).
